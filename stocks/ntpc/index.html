Understand These Numbers

Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio
Price investors pay for each ₹1 of recent annual earnings per share.
Price-to-Book (P/B) Ratio
Compares the current share price to the accounting net asset value per share.
Return on Equity (ROE)
Measures how efficiently the company generated net profit on shareholder equity.
Debt to Equity
Total borrowings relative to net worth; not applicable to banks or lending institutions.
