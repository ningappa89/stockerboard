Stockerboard data sources and freshness

Full transparency into where Stockerboard data originates, how pipelines ingest official exchange and corporate filings, and how freshness is maintained.

Effective: October 2026 • Stockerboard Trust & Transparency Architecture

Data Provenance Overview

Stockerboard operates an independent quantitative data processing engine. We prioritize source verifiability and reproducible data contracts over low-latency estimates.

Data Lineage & Ingestion

Understanding Multi-Date Freshness

An important Stockerboard trust feature is that different financial metrics carry different natural timestamps:

  • Market Data Date: Reflects the latest processed eligible trading session settled by the National Stock Exchange of India.
  • Financial Reporting Date: Reflects the latest processed eligible reported financial period (e.g., FY2025, Q1 FY2026). Companies report earnings weeks after a quarter ends; Stockerboard timestamps financial records to the certified report period.
  • Corporate Action Date: Reflects the effective date or announcement date depending on the displayed field.

Two metrics displayed beside each other can legitimately have different underlying dates. Stockerboard presents these dates transparently rather than creating artificial synchronization.

Known Limitations & Disclosures

  • End-of-Day Settlement Only: Stockerboard is an analytical research platform and does not stream real-time tick-by-tick prices or low-latency intraday feeds. All equity data is compiled on an End-of-Day (EOD) closing basis.
  • Varying Reporting Periods: Different companies report on different dates and fiscal calendars, meaning comparison across companies may reflect different underlying quarterly or annual periods.
  • Unavailable Values: Loss-making companies can lack meaningful positive P/E ratios, and newly listed securities may lack complete historical periods. Unavailable values are explicitly rendered as — Not available and never silently estimated.
  • Corporate Restatements: When companies restate past accounts or adjust share counts following stock splits, bonus issues, or rights offerings, historical per-share metrics are updated in subsequent pipeline cycles.
  • Coverage Universe: The canonical coverage universe comprises active equity listings on the National Stock Exchange of India. Suspended, de-listed, or debt instruments are excluded from active screener matrices.
